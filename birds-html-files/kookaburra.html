Laughing Kookaburra

Dacelo novaeguineae

The Laughing Kookaburra is native to the east coast of Australia. A carnivorous bird known for its distinct call, which sounds like a person laughing.

Appearance

The Laughing Kookaburra is a large kingfisher with brown plumage on its back and wings, and a cream-white underbelly and head. They have a bright blue accent on their wings, and a brown spot behind their eyes. Their tails have a brown and black barred pattern.

They are best known for their call, or their "laugh". They use their call to establish territory amongst their family groups. The laugh is a learned behaviour, passed down from their parents. Kookaburras raised in captivity do not do the same laugh.

Their laugh consists of 5 main parts:

  1. "Kooa"
  2. "Cackle"
  3. "Rolling"
  4. "Ha-ha"
  5. Depending on the sex, "Go-go" (Males), or "Gurgle" (Females)
